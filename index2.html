Welcome to Madrid

Top Attractions

The Royal Palace

Visit the grand Royal Palace, the official residence of the Spanish Royal Family, known for its stunning architecture and beautiful gardens.

The Prado Museum

Explore one of the finest art museums in the world, home to an extensive collection of European art from the 12th to the early 20th century.

Retiro Park

Relax and stroll through the lush greenery of Retiro Park, a peaceful oasis in the heart of Madrid, perfect for a leisurely afternoon.

Plaza Mayor

Marvel at the historic Plaza Mayor, a central square surrounded by beautiful architecture and bustling cafes. It’s a perfect spot for people-watching.
